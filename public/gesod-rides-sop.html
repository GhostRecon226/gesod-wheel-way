It is written for the team members who use the admin dashboard, and covers what customers can see and do on their side where it affects how you should work.

01

Platform Overview

GESOD RIDES manages the full journey of a vehicle from a US auction yard to a customer's doorstep in Nigeria — sourcing, bidding, inland trucking, ocean freight, customs clearance, and final delivery, plus every invoice and payment along the way, all from one system.

The Two Sides of the Platform

Customer-Facing
Customer Portal (Nigeria)
Browse auction listings, request quotes and bids, track a vehicle by VIN, view invoices and payment history, and raise disputes.
Staff-Facing
Admin Portal (US Logistics Operations)
Run the full pipeline — vehicle intake, trucking (Loads), drivers, ocean freight schedules, customs milestones, invoicing, documents, and customer communication.

User Types

The platform has two account roles: Admin and Customer. On login, the platform looks up the account's role and routes it automatically — admins land in the admin dashboard, customers in their own dashboard. There's no manual "switch view"; the role decides everything the account can see and do.

02

Getting Started

Every session starts the same way, whether you're staff or a customer.

Logging In

  1. Go to www.gesodrides.com and select Sign In, or navigate directly to /login.
  2. Enter your email and password. If you've forgotten your password, use Forgot password? to receive a reset link by email.
  3. New accounts must confirm their email before their first login — if confirmation is still pending, a Resend Confirmation Email button appears automatically on a failed attempt.
  4. On success, the platform reads your account's role and redirects you straight to /dashboard/admin or /dashboard/customer.

Admin Dashboard Overview

The admin dashboard is a fixed left sidebar plus a main content panel. The sidebar carries the GESOD RIDES logo at the top, the full list of modules below it, and your name with a Logout button at the bottom. On mobile, the sidebar collapses behind a menu icon.

Sidebar Navigation

Overview Import Pipeline Customers Vehicles Loads Drivers Invoices Auction Listings Bid Requests Quote Requests Documents Payments Disputes Sailing Schedules Notifications Messages Reports & Exports

The Overview tab is the landing screen and shows five live counters at a glance: Vehicles In Transit, Pending Bids, Pending Quotes, Open Disputes, and Awaiting Customs — a quick daily read on what needs attention.

Most modules are simple in-page tabs with no distinct web address. Loads, Drivers, and Invoices are the exception — they have real URLs (e.g. /dashboard/admin/loads/:id), so a specific load, driver, or invoice can be bookmarked or shared as a direct link.

03

Customer Management

The Customers tab lists every customer account — name, email, phone, and a live count of vehicles linked to them, newest account first.

Create an Account

  1. Click Add Customer and enter their full name, email, and phone number.
  2. Choose how they'll get access:
    • Set temporary password — a one-time password is generated and shown on screen once. Copy it and share it with the customer directly; it will not be shown again.
    • Send invite email — the customer receives an email and sets their own password from the link inside it.
  3. Click Create. The account is created through a dedicated server-side function, so credentials are always handled securely — never typed into the database directly.

Find an Existing Customer

The list is sorted by most recently added. There's no dedicated search box on this tab — use your browser's find-in-page (Ctrl/Cmd+F) to jump to a name or email quickly.

View Vehicles / History

Click the number in a customer's Vehicles column to open a page listing every vehicle linked to their account (year/make/model, VIN, status). Click ← Back to customers to return to the list. Use Edit on any row to update an existing customer's name, email, or phone.

04

Auction Listings

The Auction Listings tab shows every listing GESOD RIDES has published — thumbnail, make/model/year, lot number, source, auction date, status, and Edit/Delete actions.

Add a Listing Manually

Click Add Listing and complete the form, grouped into three sections:

Group 1
Vehicle
Make, model, year, VIN, body style, engine, transmission, drivetrain, fuel type, exterior/interior colour, odometer.
Group 2
Condition & Value
Title type (Clean / Salvage / Rebuilt / Certificate of Destruction), estimated retail value, primary/secondary damage, damage description, run-and-drive and keys-available checkboxes.
Group 3
Auction
Lot number, auction source (e.g. Copart, IAAI), auction date, yard location, status (Active/Expired).

Photos are uploaded separately below the form. The first photo becomes the listing's cover image; photos can be reordered or removed before saving.

AI Import — Paste a Copart/IAAI URL

  1. Above the listings table, paste the vehicle's URL from Copart or IAAI into the Import from auction link box and click Import.
  2. The platform fetches the page and uses AI to read out the vehicle's details and photos, then opens the same listing form pre-filled for review. Nothing is published until you click Save.
  3. If the auction site blocks the automatic fetch, use Link blocked? Paste page details instead — open the auction page yourself, select all (Ctrl/Cmd+A), copy, and paste the raw text in. The same AI extraction runs on the pasted content.
  4. Any field the AI couldn't determine is flagged in an amber notice at the top of the form — fill those in by hand before saving.

How Listings Appear Publicly

Any listing with status Active shows on the public Listings page, where customers can browse it and submit a Bid Request (see Section 9).

How Expired Listings Are Handled

Not a scheduled job — happens live There is no background process that expires listings on a timer. Every time the Auction Listings tab (or the public Listings/Archive pages) loads, the platform compares each active listing's auction date against today's date. Any active listing whose auction date has already passed is flipped to Expired and moved onto the public Archive page automatically, with no admin action required. The archive carries a notice that GESOD RIDES doesn't own these vehicles and can't accept new bids on closed auctions.

Vehicle & VIN Management

The Vehicles tab is separate from Auction Listings: a Listing is the public-facing item a customer can bid on before purchase; a Vehicle record is the operational record for a vehicle GESOD RIDES is actively handling for a customer, tracked by VIN from bid through delivery.

Create a Vehicle Record

From the Vehicles tab, add the vehicle's VIN, make, model, year, title type, damage description, odometer, run-and-drive status, and auction details (source, lot number, yard location, auction date).

Assign to Customer

Use the customer dropdown on the vehicle record to link it to the customer's account, or leave it unassigned until one is confirmed.

Update Milestones — All 16 Stages

To log progress: open the vehicle record, choose the current stage from the list below, add optional notes, and save. Each entry is added to the vehicle's history — it's a running log, not a single status flag, so the full timeline stays visible to both admin and customer.

Documents & Evidence Photos

Attach supporting documents to a vehicle from its record — these are reviewed separately (see Section 15, Documents). Attach an evidence photo to a specific milestone entry so the customer sees visual proof of that stage on the public tracker.

Public VIN Tracker

The full vehicle record is never exposed publicly. The public Track page (no login required) calls a dedicated, restricted lookup that returns only a masked VIN (first 4 and last 4 characters, middle hidden) and the milestone timeline for an exact, full 17-character VIN match — nothing else about the vehicle or its owner is returned. Searches are capped at 5 per hour per browser to prevent abuse.

The same masking applies everywhere a VIN is shown inside the admin panel's lists and tables (Vehicles, Documents, Loads, Import Pipeline, etc.) — the full VIN is only visible inside the edit form itself.

06

Loads (US Operations)

A Load is the US-side trucking record — moving a specific vehicle from an auction/storage yard to its destination. It's tracked by its own VIN and is separate from the Vehicle/milestone record used for the Nigeria-facing shipping pipeline, though both usually exist for the same vehicle.

Create a New Load

From the Loads tab, click New Load and enter the VIN — make/model/year auto-fill once a full 17-character VIN is entered, though this can always be overridden manually — plus lot number, buyer number, pickup location, destination type (Port / Yard / Container / Residence) and address, agreed pickup price, and service fee (defaults to $50).

Assign a Driver

Pick from the Driver dropdown on the load form, or leave unassigned and assign later. The Loads list can also be filtered by driver, customer, or status.

Update Status Through Its Stages

Open a load and choose Update Status. A load moves through:

Posted→ Driver Assigned→ Picked Up→ In Transit→ Delivered→ Invoiced→ Paid

Every status change is timestamped and saved to that load's status history, with optional notes — the full progression stays auditable.

Import from Excel/CSV

  1. Click Import Loads and drag in a .xlsx, .xls, or .csv tracking spreadsheet.
  2. The platform previews the first 5 rows and auto-detects which spreadsheet column maps to which load field. VIN must be mapped to continue.
  3. Review and adjust the column mapping, then review every row — rows missing a VIN are flagged and excluded automatically.
  4. Uncheck any row you don't want imported, then confirm. Valid rows are created as new loads in one batch, and you're shown how many were created versus skipped.
07

Driver Management

Add a Driver

From the Drivers tab, click Add Driver and enter name and phone (required), plus optional email, payment method, payment details (e.g. a Zelle number or ACH details), and notes. Drivers can be marked Active/Inactive; inactive drivers are hidden from the list by default — toggle Show inactive drivers to see them.

Record a Payment

Open a driver's detail page and click Record Payment. Choose one of that driver's delivered loads, enter the amount, payment method, and date, with optional notes — the payment is recorded as pending until confirmed.

View Outstanding Payments

A driver's detail page shows Total Paid and Total Pending at a glance, plus full payment history with a one-click Mark as Paid on any pending row. For a company-wide view, open Payments Overview from the Drivers tab — it lists every pending driver payment across all drivers (driver, load VIN, customer, amount, method, days pending), with a bulk Mark Selected as Paid action and a running total of what's outstanding.

Quote Requests

Customer Submission

Customers submit a Quote Request from their dashboard when they want pricing on a vehicle or service before committing.

View & Respond

The Quote Requests tab lists every request. Click Edit on a row to respond inline.

Issue a Quote — USD and NGN

Set the amount in USD and its equivalent in NGN (₦), a "valid until" date, and admin notes, then update the status:

Pending→ Issued→ Accepted or Expired
09

Bid Requests

Customer Submission from Listings

Customers submit a Bid Request directly from an active Auction Listing, specifying their maximum bid.

Approve / Reject / Update Outcomes

The Bid Requests tab lists every request. Click Edit on a row, then choose a status and add admin notes. There's a single unified status field rather than separate approve/reject buttons, so any status can be set at any time as the bid progresses:

Pending→ Approved / Rejected→ Won / Lost

Invoicing

Create an Invoice

  1. Pick a customer — the platform automatically finds their delivered, not-yet-invoiced loads.
  2. Select loads — choose which of those loads to include on this invoice.
  3. Review line items — a Base Pickup Price and Service Fee line are generated automatically for each selected load. Add or adjust extra charges as needed.
  4. Review & save — check the full breakdown and grand total, add optional notes, and save. This creates the invoice as a Draft, links the selected loads to it, and moves those loads' status to invoiced.

Add Line Items / Extra Charges

Beyond the automatic Base Pickup Price and Service Fee, extra charge types available are:

Tire Change Battery Key Fix Repair Surcharge Other

Draft → Approved → Sent → Paid Workflow

Draft→ Approved→ Sent→ Paid

Every invoice starts as a Draft and moves through each stage as a deliberate admin action from the invoice's detail page.

Why Customers Cannot See Draft Invoices

Enforced twice, not once A customer's invoice list excludes any invoice with status Draft — enforced both by the database's own access rules and again in the app itself, so a draft can never appear to a customer under any circumstance, even briefly. Only once an invoice moves out of Draft does it become visible to the customer it belongs to.
11

Payments

Record a Payment Against a Vehicle

From the Payments tab, click Add Payment, choose the customer, optionally link it to a specific vehicle, note the payment stage (free text, e.g. "Auction Deposit"), amount, and currency (USD or NGN). It's recorded as pending.

Confirm a Payment

Click Confirm on any pending payment row — this records which admin confirmed it and flips its status to confirmed.

Customer Payment History View

Customers see their own payment records from their dashboard, view-only — they cannot record or confirm payments themselves.

Disputes

Customer Filing

Customers file a dispute from their dashboard against a vehicle or order.

Admin Response

The Disputes tab lists every dispute. Click Respond to open a reply form — write the response and optionally attach evidence (an image or PDF).

Resolve / Close

Change the status alongside your response:

Open→ Under Review→ Resolved

Sailing Schedules

Add a New Schedule

From the Sailing Schedules tab, click Add Schedule and enter vessel name, departure port, ETD (estimated departure date), ETA Nigeria, destination port (Apapa, Tin Can, or Onne), and status (Scheduled / Departed / Arrived).

Public Schedule Page Display

The public Sailing Schedule page lists every schedule sorted soonest-departure-first, with the same vessel, port, and date columns, so any visitor can check upcoming and recent vessel departures without logging in.

Notifications

Send to One Customer

On the Notifications tab, select a specific customer from the dropdown and write the message.

Send to All Customers

Check Send to all customers instead of picking one — the message is delivered to every customer account in a single action. Either way, the notification appears in that customer's (or every customer's) dashboard notification list.

Documents

Upload to Vehicle

Documents (title, bill of sale, customs paperwork, etc.) are uploaded and attached to a specific vehicle record.

Review & Customer Access

Pending→ Approved / Rejected

Customers only ever see documents attached to their own vehicles, and see the current review status reflected on each one.

18

Important Rules

A handful of rules are built into the platform itself and cannot be overridden from the admin panel. They're worth knowing precisely.

Invoicing

Draft invoices are never visible to customers.

This is enforced twice — once by the database's own access rules, and again in the app itself — so a draft can never appear to a customer, under any circumstance.

Data Access

Customers only ever see their own data.

Their own vehicles, invoices, payments, documents, and disputes — never another customer's, regardless of how the data is requested.

VIN Tracking

VIN tracking is public, but VINs are always masked.

The public tracker shows only the first 4 and last 4 characters of a VIN, and never exposes any other private vehicle or customer detail.

Auction Listings

Listings expire automatically once their auction date passes.

This happens live, whenever listings are loaded — not on a fixed schedule — and requires no admin action.
